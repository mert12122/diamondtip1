Directions

  1. Choose a diamond tip size, then drag the force slider to set the pressure.
  2. Raise the temperature slowly. Use the +10 button as you get close.
  3. Watch the X-ray detector. When the sharp rings disappear, the iron has melted.
  4. Click Record Melting Point.
  5. Click Reset Sample, set a new pressure, and repeat. Record at least five trials at different pressures.
  6. Complete Explore 4 in your Investigation Journal.
